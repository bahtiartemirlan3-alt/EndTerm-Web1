Rental Rules & Policies

Please review all guidelines below prior to finalizing your booking.

1. Driver Requirements

  • Minimum age of 21 required (young driver fee applies under 25).
  • Valid domestic or international driver's license held for at least 1 year.
  • Major credit card in the primary driver's name required for security deposit.
  • $300 refundable deposit held at the time of pick-up.
